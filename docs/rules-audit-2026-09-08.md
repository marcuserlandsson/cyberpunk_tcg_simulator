## Official ambiguities to retain visibly

- **C01 — selling:** 5.8.4 permits arbitrary effect-driven sales independently of the once-per-turn main action, while 11.9.2.2 says an effect saying Sell counts as that action. Preserve the explicit area/action distinction provisionally and label it as an interpretation until official clarification; do not claim confirmed compliance for this interaction.
- **C02 — Quick location:** 11.26.1.2 says field, whereas 5.7.6 and 8.14 permit face-up Legend abilities in the Legends area. Read general Quick timing together with the Legend-area permission, retaining this note.
- **C03 — Go Solo and other Legend exits:** 11.25 removes a Go Solo Legend when it leaves the field, while 4.4 allows field/Legends area generally. Track whether Go Solo was used so its stronger exit instruction applies specifically.

No message has been sent to the publisher. These notes distinguish a documented interpretation from an official ruling.
